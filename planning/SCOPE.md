# Scope

## Must have
- known distributed failure
- normalized execution evidence
- incident propagation graph
- immutable Replay Capsule
- isolated baseline replay
- at least one controlled intervention
- baseline/counterfactual comparison
- causal-role explanation
- usable visualization/demo

## Should have
- multiple intervention types
- two System Packs or two failure variants
- replay history
- capsule integrity metadata
- concise confidence/evidence explanation

## Could have
- richer automated state minimization
- advanced graph reduction
- multi-variable experiment planning
- AI-assisted hypothesis generation
- exportable incident reports

## Explicitly out of scope for hackathon MVP
- arbitrary production environment replay
- perfect deterministic replay of every distributed system
- mathematically proven general causal inference
- full observability-platform replacement
- unrestricted production side effects
